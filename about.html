About Azhagu Mayil Collection

Azhagu Mayil Collection brings together timeless Indian tradition and contemporary elegance. Our collection is carefully curated for women who appreciate beautiful fabrics, detailed craftsmanship and sophisticated designs.

Elegance of the Peacock (Mayil)

Inspired by the grace and splendour of the South Indian peacock, our sarees are crafted by generational master weavers in Kanchipuram and Varanasi.

From traditional Korvai temple borders to sheer organza drapes, each piece undergoes rigorous quality certification to ensure only authentic pure mulberry silks and tested gold/silver zari are delivered to your doorstep.
